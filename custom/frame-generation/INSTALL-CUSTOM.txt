Loading-background continuity repair
-----------------------------------
After each submitted game frame, the next writable texture inherits that frame
instead of exposing an older viewpoint during OpenMW loading-screen readbacks.
No engine executable change is required. This adds one GPU image copy per native
frame (RGBA8, current window resolution), with no CPU pixel readback. Performance
cost on your GPU is unmeasured. Keep your INI and lua51.dll unchanged.
